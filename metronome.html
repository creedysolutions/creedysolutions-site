Online metronome

Set a tempo or tap one in, pick the time signature, hit Start. It runs on the browser's audio clock, so it stays steady even when the page is busy.

100 BPM

A metronome is great for practice. On stage, a lot of bands can't run one at all: no in-ears means nobody can hear the click, and plenty of drummers would rather push the chorus anyway. If your keys, delays or loops still need a tempo, DrumClock flips it around. The drummer counts it in, and the gear follows the drummer instead of a click.
