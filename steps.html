EVERY CLICK, NUMBERED

Before and after, for each step

Steps records each click as a numbered step with a picture before the click and a picture of the result. The reader sees what to click and what should happen next.

CAPTIONS WRITTEN FOR YOU

The label is the caption

The caption comes from the label of what you clicked: Click Save, Open Stage. No typing up what you just did.

MASKED BY DEFAULT

Record data stays out of the pictures

Field values are covered in every picture by default, so customer names, amounts and the rest of your record data do not end up in training material. Add your own masks wherever you want more covered.

The page editor: arrange it, mark it up, hand it over.

Lay the steps out on pages, draw on the pictures, and export the format the reader wants.

PAGES

1, 2 or 4 steps per page

Arrange steps on pages sized as a slide, Letter or A4, with 1, 2 or 4 steps on each.

DRAWING TOOLS

Highlighter, pen and eraser

Point at the thing that matters with a highlighter or a pen, rub it out with the eraser, and add your own masks. Undo when you change your mind.

EXPORTS

PowerPoint, PDF, HTML and Markdown

HTML and Markdown are in the free version. PRO adds PowerPoint and PDF, laid out the way you arranged the pages.

Everything stays in the browser.

No account, no cloud, and nothing installed in your org.

  • Pictures stay in the extension's memory. They live there until you export them, and they are gone when the panel closes. Nothing is uploaded, because there is nowhere to upload it to.
  • No Salesforce API calls. Steps looks at the page you are on to see what was clicked and to cover field values. It does not query your org.
  • No accounts, and nothing about your org on our servers. The only server Steps ever contacts is our license server. Activating a PRO key carries just the key and a random install ID.
  • No analytics. We don't know when you install it, open it, or what you record.
  • Nothing installed in your org. No connected app, no OAuth, no package.
