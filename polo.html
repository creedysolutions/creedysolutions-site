COMPARE

One click, every org

Focus a field, flow, class, rule, layout, or page in Setup. Polo compares it against every org you're logged into and shows a verdict chip per org — tap one for the exact differences, in sentences, not raw XML.

TATTLE TRAIL

Who changed it, and where first

Setup Audit Trail, flow versions, and Polo's own snapshots merged into one history per org. It's deployment-aware: prod changing after sandbox is normal; prod changing on its own is a red banner.

PIPELINE

The whole pipeline in one matrix

Components × orgs versus a baseline: same, differs, missing, extra. Watchlist scope for your release, or object scope for a full inventory sweep.

WATCHLIST

Watch it, re-check it, ship it

Pin what's in flight. One-click dependency scan pulls in everything it needs. Daily re-check with a badge when something drifts. Export a package.xml or a print-ready release report.

DEEPER

Object scans, FLS, perm sets

Scan a whole object across orgs. Diff one field's read/edit across every profile and permission set. Compare a permission set's CRUD grid. Flow version-to-version diff, even inside one org.

Explore in 3D

Text first, 3D second: names and verdicts are always readable, the space is just how you move through them.

  • Glow is the metadata type. Look down on the disc and you see the shape of your release — how much is fields, how much is Apex, how much is flow.
  • Edges are the verdict. Gold differs, red missing — the missing pill pulses.
  • Filters are the key. Toggle severity and type chips; the disc rebuilds.
  • Dive into history. The front card is the top of a deck of snapshots. Scroll and it slides away, revealing the past — the green one matches prod.

Read-only isn't a setting. It's the product.

Polo never writes to an org, and your metadata never leaves your browser.

  • No deploys, ever. Polo emits a package.xml and a ready-to-run sf command — you run the retrieve or deploy. Polo's own network layer is hard-wired to GET.
  • No servers, no accounts. The only calls are from your browser to your own orgs' APIs, using the sessions you already have.
  • No telemetry. We don't know when you install it, open it, or what orgs you point it at.
  • Nothing installed in your org. No connected app, no OAuth, no package.
